Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. YouTube API Services

Clipping4Gaming uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to YouTube. By using Clipping4Gaming, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Information we access

When you sign in with Google, Clipping4Gaming requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos that you have reviewed and approved to your own YouTube channel. Clipping4Gaming does not read your e-mail, contacts, files, watch history, comments, subscribers or any other data from your Google or YouTube account.

3. How information is used and stored

  • The OAuth access token issued by Google is stored only on the computer where Clipping4Gaming is installed (file youtube_token.json).
  • Videos, titles and descriptions are created and stored locally on that computer before upload.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

4. Sharing of information

Clipping4Gaming does not sell, rent or share any user information with internal or external third parties. Uploaded videos are published on YouTube according to the visibility you choose.

5. Cookies

Clipping4Gaming is a desktop tool. It does not place, access or recognize cookies or similar technologies on your device or browser. This website does not use cookies.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke Clipping4Gaming's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

7. Data deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting the youtube_token.json file and the queue folder on your computer. If you revoke access, Clipping4Gaming can no longer use the token. Clipping4Gaming does not keep any copy of your data elsewhere. For any request, contact us at the address below; we answer within 7 days and delete any data we might hold within 30 days.
