Clipping4Gaming

Clipping4Gaming is a personal desktop tool that turns long GTA 6 gameplay recordings into short vertical clips (9:16, with subtitles) and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. The channel owner places gameplay recordings that they recorded themselves or have permission to use into a local folder.
  2. The tool detects highlight moments and cuts short vertical clips on the owner's computer.
  3. The owner reviews every clip manually.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own channel using the YouTube API Services.
